Your first five minutes

From installer to playing a movie off your own channel.

Install & open

Run the installer. On Windows, first launch may show the SmartScreen note above. That's normal for indie apps without an EV certificate.

Let the wizard log you in

It opens my.telegram.org in a built-in window, fills the app-creation form for you, and saves your API keys locally. Then scan the QR with Telegram. Done.

You do this once. Sessions persist across restarts; logout wipes them from disk.

Build your library

Create a folder (it's a real channel behind the scenes), rename existing ones with [NB], or paste a public-channel link. Press play on anything.
